# Before/follow-up video standards

The homepage currently uses generated poster frames to demonstrate layout only. They are not testimonials and do not depict a product outcome.

## Production media slots

When authentic owner footage is approved, add paired files under `public/videos/canis/` and replace the poster-only `StoryFrame` implementation with native `<video>` elements.

Recommended pair:

- `story-01-before.mp4`
- `story-01-followup.mp4`

Keep a poster image for each clip so mobile pages load quickly.

## Intake requirements

- Written owner permission covering the dog, owner, footage, quotation, and advertising use
- Original source files with capture dates retained privately
- Same dog, comparable movement task, and materially similar camera angle
- No speed changes, frame removal, or edits that alter perceived gait or mobility
- No fabricated chronology or attribution
- Clear disclosure of compensation, gifted product, affiliate status, or other relationship
- Veterinary and legal review before publication

## Presentation requirements

- Prefer “before” and “follow-up” over a guaranteed “result” framing
- State the actual elapsed time only when source dates verify it
- Describe owner observation as anecdotal and individual
- Do not imply that RUO material was administered or that it caused the observed change
- Do not present stories as evidence of safety, efficacy, diagnosis, treatment, cure, or prevention
- Keep the research-use-only and veterinary-advice disclaimers adjacent to the media

The approved path to stronger claims is controlled canine evidence, not more aggressive testimonial editing.
