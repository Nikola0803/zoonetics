# ZooNetics logo assets

- `print/zoonetics-primary.svg` — primary full-color logo on transparent background.
- `print/zoonetics-by-evlv.svg` — endorsed full-color lockup on transparent background.
- `print/zoonetics-primary-4200px.png` — transparent high-resolution primary logo.
- `print/zoonetics-by-evlv-4200px.png` — transparent high-resolution endorsed lockup.
- `zoonetics-primary-reversed.svg` — reversed logo for dark digital backgrounds.
- `zoonetics-by-evlv-reversed.svg` — reversed endorsed lockup for dark digital backgrounds.

The print files are resolution-independent SVG vectors with all letterforms drawn as paths. They contain no linked fonts or raster artwork and can be scaled for vial labels, cartons, signage, or print production without loss of quality.

Maintain clear space equal to the height of the coral dot on every side. Do not stretch, recolor individual letters, or remove the coral dot or blue underline.
