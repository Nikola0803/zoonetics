# ZooNetics logo assets

- `print/zoonetics-primary.svg` — dark-letter vector logo for light backgrounds.
- `print/zoonetics-by-evlv.svg` — dark-letter endorsed vector lockup for light backgrounds.
- `print/zoonetics-primary-4200px.png` — dark-letter transparent high-resolution primary logo.
- `print/zoonetics-by-evlv-4200px.png` — dark-letter transparent high-resolution endorsed lockup.
- `zoonetics-primary-reversed.svg` — light-letter vector logo for dark backgrounds.
- `zoonetics-by-evlv-reversed.svg` — light-letter endorsed vector lockup for dark backgrounds.
- `print/zoonetics-primary-light-4200px.png` — light-letter transparent high-resolution primary logo.
- `print/zoonetics-by-evlv-light-4200px.png` — light-letter transparent high-resolution endorsed lockup.
- `print/evlv-logo-dark-exact.png` — cropped directly from the supplied dark EVLV artwork; no redraw.
- `print/evlv-logo-white-exact.png` — cropped directly from the supplied white EVLV artwork; no redraw.

The print files are resolution-independent SVG vectors with all letterforms drawn as paths. They contain no linked fonts or raster artwork and can be scaled for vial labels, cartons, signage, or print production without loss of quality.

The endorsement uses a deliberately small lowercase `by` followed by the exact supplied EVLV artwork. The endorsed SVG keeps that original raster artwork linked from the same print folder, while the 4200px PNG exports are flattened and self-contained. Maintain clear space equal to the height of the coral dot on every side. Do not stretch, recolor individual letters, or remove the coral dot or straight blue rule.
